# REQ-076 — Billing and cancellation are self-service, and cancelling keeps everything running to the paid-through date

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want to update my card, find my invoices, and cancel
without asking anyone, so that leaving is as easy as the pricing card promised
and I know exactly what I keep until when.

## Rationale
The product sells on "Cancel in one click", so the customer must be able to
update their card, find their invoices and leave without asking anyone, must be
told the exact date their access ends, and must keep everything they paid for
until that date — a cancellation that needs an email, or that cuts service off
mid-period, is a broken public promise rather than a billing detail (BUILD.md
§13, "Portal for card/cancel; cancel keeps access to period end; export
always"; §4.7, "Cancel plan + 'cancelling keeps everything running until
{date}'"). What a cancelled customer keeps is their content, which is REQ-078's;
how long the hosted CMS goes on serving their published pages, what a visitor
gets once it stops, and who is mailed about it, is REQ-059's — this requirement
owns only what the cancellation screen itself says at the moment they cancel.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when they open billing, then they see their
   plan and price, the date and amount of the next invoice, the card on file,
   and a way to reach their invoices.
2. Given the customer chooses to update their card, change the address their
   invoices and receipts go to, add or correct the VAT number their invoices
   carry, or view invoices, when they proceed, then they reach Stripe's own
   customer portal and return to Settings afterwards still signed in. That
   portal is where all four changes are made — each theirs without contacting
   anyone — and no other surface in the product offers a field for any of the
   four; what the VAT number is recorded against and how it reaches an invoice
   is REQ-023 criteria 1 and 2.
3. Given the customer cancels, when the cancellation is confirmed, then the
   screen states the exact date their access ends, and measurement, generation
   and publishing continue unchanged until that date, except that no page is
   generated whose publish moment — the moment REQ-057 criterion 2 actually
   publishes it, not the date it was planned for — would fall after that date.
4. Given a cancelled subscription, when that date passes, then no further page
   is generated, published or charged for; any draft still in review can no
   longer be approved, is never published afterwards, and stays exportable
   (REQ-078); and pages already published stay live — those on the customer's
   own destination indefinitely, those on the hosted CMS for as long as REQ-059
   criterion 6 goes on serving them.
5. Given the date has passed, when the customer requests a sign-in link at
   their address, then it is sent, signing in works, and Settings still offers
   their content export (REQ-078), their billing details and a way to resume.
6. Given a cancelled customer changes their mind, when they resume — before or
   after the paid-through date — then they can do so themselves without
   contacting anyone, onto the same domain, category and competitor set they
   left with.
7. Given the customer cancels, when the cancellation is confirmed, then the
   screen tells them that their content export stays available afterwards with
   no end date (REQ-078) whatever happens to their published pages, and — where
   any of their pages are served on the hosted CMS — the date those pages stop
   being served (REQ-059 criterion 6).
8. Given any site, when the weekly re-measurement and the daily generation and
   publishing loops ask whether it has active access (REQ-065 criterion 1),
   then it has active access while its paid-through
   date has not passed and none once that date has passed, whether or not the
   subscription was cancelled and whether or not its latest renewal was paid.

## Non-goals
- Checkout and the first payment, which happen before an account exists
  (REQ-020, BUILD §13).
- Stripe Tax, VAT registration or invoicing compliance — deferred by owner
  ruling (BUILD §13). Criterion 2 owns the surface on which the customer
  corrects their VAT number and nothing else about tax; what the amount payable
  is and whether tax sits inside it is REQ-022's, and nothing here calculates,
  charges or remits tax.
- Plan changes: there is one plan at €49/mo, with no upgrades, downgrades,
  annual billing, seats or add-ons.
- Credits, pauses or discount codes.
- Refunds, chargebacks and a failed renewal (`past_due`): deferred to
  post-launch by owner ruling, an accepted debt on the same footing as the tax
  deferral (BUILD §13), to be revisited before meaningful EU B2C volume. No
  criterion here states how a failed payment is retried or what the customer is
  told while it is, and none is written until that revisit; criterion 8 states
  only the access gate the weekly and daily loops read.
- What a visitor gets from a hosted page after it stops being served, and the
  serving behaviour itself — REQ-059's.
- Which customers are mailed about the end of hosted serving, on what occasions,
  and what that mail says — REQ-059 criterion 7's, over REQ-064's registry of
  kinds. This requirement owns only what the cancellation screen itself tells the
  customer at the moment they cancel.
- How the portal is configured, which fields it renders and where each sits on
  it: criterion 2 fixes that the portal is the surface and that all four changes
  are the customer's to make there. Enabling the fields is the blueprint's.
