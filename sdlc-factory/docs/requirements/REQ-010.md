# REQ-010 — A finished page is the trade for an email address

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who now knows I am missing from my market's biggest searches, I
want the first page you would write for me sent to my inbox, so that I leave
with something publishable rather than a diagnosis.

## Rationale
A founder must leave with something publishable rather than a diagnosis, and the
exchange must be explicit in both directions: the page is written only once an
email address has been given, the rest of the report is never held back to force
the trade, and an address once given is always honoured or the founder is told
it was not. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.2 (the giveaway email) and §4.1 (free page
card).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report for which at least one page was found worth writing,
   when it renders, then it shows the title of the first page ReachKit would
   write, the search that page targets, and one control to have the full page
   emailed — a control that asks for an email address and nothing else: no
   account, no password, no payment, no further field.
2. Given no email address has been submitted for a report, when the report is
   viewed for any length of time, then no full page is written for it.
3. Given a visitor who submits their email address, when they submit, then the
   full page is written and delivered to that address, and the page is delivered
   to no other address. That delivery is a mail the founder cannot stop: it is
   the thing they asked for, sent once, in answer to their own submission.
4. Given the delivered email, when it is opened, then it contains the complete
   page in a copy-ready form, the target search with its monthly volume, and a
   line stating this is the first of the pages found for that domain.
5. Given a visitor who never submits an email address, when they use the report,
   then every other part of it remains fully readable.
6. Given a scan that derived no page worth writing, when the report renders,
   then it states in writing that no page is offered for this domain and shows
   no control to have one emailed; no page is invented to fill the slot.
7. Given an email address the report accepted, when the page cannot be written
   or the mail cannot be sent, then no confirmation of delivery is shown or sent
   for a page that was not sent, and the founder is told the page is not coming
   and why, in a written statement naming the cause: in writing where they
   submitted it if they are still on the report, and otherwise by one written
   message to the address they gave — a message they cannot stop, because it
   closes the request they made.
8. Given mail to the address the founder gave is itself what failed, when that
   failure is handled, then what is retried is whichever of the two things they
   are owed exists: the page itself where the page was written, and criterion
   7's message where it was not. Delivery to that address is retried for 24
   hours from the first attempt; if nothing has been delivered by then, no
   further attempt is made and nothing is shown or recorded as delivered.

## Non-goals
- No account creation, password, or payment as part of the trade — stated as
  criterion 1, not left to this list.
- No second free page, page library, or regeneration of the page on request.
- No images in the delivered draft (`BUILD.md` §17).
- The set of causes a failure under criterion 7 can have, and which written line
  each gets: the blueprint's, derived from this requirement's promise that the
  founder is told both that the page is not coming and why.
