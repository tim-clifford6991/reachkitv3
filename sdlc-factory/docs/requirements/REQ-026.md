# REQ-026 — The founder confirms the market at setup

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder at setup, I want to see the market we think I am in and correct it,
so that everything measured and written for me is aimed at my actual buyers.

## Rationale
The market is the root of everything downstream — the search set, the questions,
the rivals, and every page written from them — so a wrong one makes the whole
product wrong in a way the founder cannot see later. The founder must see the
market we inferred for them, be able to replace it, and know that what they
confirmed is what the product uses; where nothing was inferred they state it
themselves rather than being shown a guess dressed as a measurement, and setup
does not proceed without one (`BUILD.md` §4.3, §6.7). What decides which of the
two they meet is whether the product holds a completed report for the address
the account will use, never how the purchase was made — the same key REQ-021
criterion 3 and REQ-027 criterion 1 already turn on — so a founder who scanned
their own site and then bought from a price surface confirms the market inferred
for it rather than retyping it. The freezing of the question set after
confirmation belongs to the measurement requirement that owns the question set.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder at setup for whose site address the product holds a completed
   report — whether or not that report was the one their purchase came from —
   when the market card renders, then it shows the one market inferred for that
   address, in plain buyer language, and the founder can change it before
   submitting.
2. Given a founder who does not recognise the market shown, when they replace
   it, then what they entered is the market the product uses from then on, and
   it is visible to them in the app afterwards.
3. Given a setup where the product holds no completed report for the site
   address the account will use — nobody having scanned it, or the buyer having
   replaced the domain their report measured, so that the account stands as one
   with nothing measured yet (REQ-090 criterion 4) — when the founder reaches
   the market card, then it is empty and asks them to state their market in
   their own words, with nothing pre-filled and nothing presented as inferred or
   measured.
4. Given the founder has submitted setup, when the deep pass runs and every time
   the product uses their market afterwards, then it is the market they
   confirmed or stated, and it changes only when they change it themselves
   (REQ-070 criterion 1) — never by re-inference, at setup or at any later
   measurement.
5. Given a founder who has stated no market, when they try to submit setup, then
   setup does not proceed and one written line says the market is needed.
6. Given a founder who changes the site address on this screen after the market
   card has rendered (REQ-090 criterion 3), when they change it, then any market
   inferred for the domain they replaced is cleared, the card then stands by the
   address they gave — criterion 1's inferred market where the product holds a
   completed report for it, criterion 3's empty card where it does not — and a
   market they typed themselves is kept exactly as they left it.

## Non-goals
- Choosing from a fixed industry taxonomy — criteria 2 and 3 carry the
  customer's promise that the market is theirs to state in their own words.
- More than one market per site.
- Per-country or non-US-English markets (MVP is US Google / `en`).
- Re-deriving the market without the founder changing it — criterion 4 carries
  it.
