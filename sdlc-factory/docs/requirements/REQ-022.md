# REQ-022 — €49 a month, VAT included, one price, nothing added

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder deciding whether to buy, I want one flat monthly price, tax
included, with nothing metered behind it, so that I know exactly what ReachKit
costs me before I pay and every month after.

## Rationale
The founder's decision rests on a number they can hold in their head: €49 a
month, VAT included, flat, with nothing metered behind it, so the product is
never something they have to ration — which is the opposite of what it is for.
The €49 is the whole of what the customer owes, so the bill they signed up to
never rises when the tax treatment behind it changes. This requirement is the
single home for the price; every surface that states it cites here (`BUILD.md`
§13, §4.1).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder on any surface that states the price, when they read it, then
   it reads €49 per month and says that VAT is included, and €49 is the amount
   checkout charges.
2. Given an active customer, when their month elapses, then €49 is charged
   again, with no per-page, per-scan, per-competitor or any other usage-based
   amount added to it, whatever they used that month.
3. Given a founder at checkout, when they look for an alternative plan, then
   there is exactly one — no tiers, no seats, no annual variant, no add-ons.
4. Given a buyer in any country, when they reach the amount payable and every
   time they are charged after, then it is €49 in euro — the same amount and the
   same currency wherever they are, never converted to a local one — with no tax
   amount added to it or taken from it, any VAT due being contained within the
   €49, so no customer's bill rises because tax registration was set up.

## Non-goals
- Annual or multi-month billing, per-seat pricing, or a second plan — criterion
  3 carries the customer's promise.
- Trials, coupons, discount codes, or promotional pricing.
- Charging in any currency other than EUR — criterion 4 carries it.
- Overage charges, credits, or metered usage of any kind — criterion 2 carries
  what may never be added to the €49.
- Calculating, displaying or remitting VAT, or showing a tax line beside the
  €49 — criteria 1 and 4 carry what the customer reads and pays; what is
  recorded for tax purposes is REQ-023's.
- Cancellation and card changes — REQ-076's, not the price's.
- Refunds, chargebacks and failed renewals: no requirement states what those
  customers experience, because REQ-076 defers all three to post-launch by owner
  ruling.
