# REQ-006 — The AI-answers card states its denominator and names who was cited instead

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has never checked what AI tells buyers, I want to see on how
many of my market's biggest searches an AI answer appears and which brands it
names, so that I can see for myself that it names competitors and not me.

## Rationale
A founder must be able to see, and check, exactly what AI tells buyers in their
market: how many of their 12 biggest searches produce an AI answer at all, which
brands those answers name, and whether they are among them — stated against a
denominator, so a search that produced no AI answer is never counted as a place
they were ignored, and so the card never claims an assistant we did not query.
Which vendor tiers supply the data is evidence, held in
`registry/evidence/REQ-006.md`. Source: `BUILD.md` §6.2 and §4.1 (AI answers
card).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the AI-answers card renders, then it states in
   writing on how many of the market's 12 biggest searches an AI answer
   appeared, and every citation count on the card is shown against that
   denominator.
2. Given a search that returned no AI answer, when the matrix renders, then that
   cell states that no AI answer appeared and is excluded from both the
   customer's citation count and its denominator.
3. Given an AI answer that cites domains, when its row renders, then the cited
   domains are named, and the customer's own row shows whether they were among
   them.
4. Given a customer cited on none of the AI answers, when the card renders, then
   that is shown as a measurement against its stated denominator — never as an
   error, a blank, or an omitted card.
5. Given no AI answer appeared on any of the 12 searches, when the card renders,
   then the card is still shown, states that AI answers appeared on 0 of the 12,
   and presents that as a measurement — never an error, an empty card, or an
   omitted section.
6. Given the card, when it renders, then one written line states that the
   answers measured are Google's AI answers to the market's 12 biggest searches,
   and no other assistant or engine is named anywhere on the card.
7. Given the card, when it renders, then it reports what was measured and
   nothing more: it carries no instruction, technique, phrasing tip or
   recommendation for changing what an AI answer names, and no control offering
   one. The only fixes the report puts in front of a founder anywhere are the
   three REQ-009 owns.

## Non-goals
- No per-engine breakdown, no ChatGPT or Perplexity result on the free report
  (`BUILD.md` §6.2, "Paid only" and "Deferred to v1.1").
- No sentiment, ranking, or share-of-voice metric derived from the citations.
- No advice on how to influence what an assistant recommends (`BUILD.md` §14) —
  stated as criterion 7, not left to this list.
