# REQ-006 — The AI-answers card states its denominator, names who was cited instead, and shows the search behind each of the 12 questions

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has never checked what AI tells buyers, I want to see on how
many of my market's biggest searches an AI answer appears, which brands it names,
and the real search and monthly volume behind each of the 12 questions, so that I
can see for myself that it names competitors and not me — and judge whether this
is actually my market before I believe the verdict.

## Rationale
A founder must be able to see, and check, exactly what AI tells buyers in their
market: how many of their 12 biggest searches produce an AI answer at all, which
brands those answers name, whether they are among them — stated against a
denominator, so a search that produced no AI answer is never counted as a place
they were ignored — and, for each of the 12, the real search and volume it came
from, 12 genuinely different searches rather than one rephrased. The 12 questions
are the foundation the whole card rests on: without the search beside each one a
founder cannot tell a measured market from a guess, and the correction path
(REQ-094) has nothing to act on. Which vendor tiers supply the data is evidence,
held in `registry/evidence/REQ-006.md`; the selection scoring, intent weights and
composition constraints are mechanism for the blueprint. Placement: JN-001 step
4. Sources: `BUILD.md` §6.2, §4.1 (AI answers card, the 12 questions list and its
provenance line) and §6.7 steps 3–4. No implementation exists to touch: this repo
carries no `src/`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the AI-answers card renders, then it states in
   writing on how many of the market's 12 biggest searches an AI answer
   appeared, and every citation count on the card is shown against that
   denominator.
2. Given a search that returned no AI answer, when the matrix renders, then that
   cell states that no AI answer appeared and is excluded from both the
   customer's citation count and its denominator.
3. Given an AI answer that cites domains, when its row renders, then the cited
   domains are named, and the customer's own row shows whether they were among
   them.
4. Given a customer cited on none of the AI answers, when the card renders, then
   that is shown as a measurement against its stated denominator — never as an
   error, a blank, or an omitted card.
5. Given no AI answer appeared on any of the 12 searches, when the card renders,
   then the card is still shown, states that AI answers appeared on 0 of the 12,
   and presents that as a measurement — never an error, an empty card, or an
   omitted section.
6. Given the card, when it renders, then one written line states that the
   answers measured are Google's AI answers to the market's 12 biggest searches,
   and no other assistant or engine is named anywhere on the card.
7. Given the card, when it renders, then it reports what was measured and
   nothing more: it carries no instruction, technique, phrasing tip or
   recommendation for changing what an AI answer names, and no control offering
   one. The only fixes the report puts in front of a founder anywhere are the
   three REQ-009 owns.
8. Given a completed report, when the questions module renders, then all 12
   questions are reachable from the page, with the first four visible and the
   remainder one action away.
9. Given any question, when it renders, then it shows the search it was derived
   from, that search's monthly search volume, and the brands named in its
   answer.
10. Given a question on which no AI answer appeared at all, when its row renders,
    then it states that no AI answer appeared, and it is never presented as the
    customer being absent from an answer.
11. Given a question whose AI answer named no brand, when its provenance line
    renders, then it states that the answer named no brand rather than showing an
    empty list.
12. Given the set of 12, when it renders, then no two of them derive from
    searches that differ only in plural form, word order, punctuation or
    stop-words — the 12 stand for 12 different searches.
13. Given any of the 12 questions, when it renders, then the search shown beside
    it (criterion 9) is the search that question was selected from, and how the
    question is worded never changes which search it stands for: a differently
    worded question is still the same measured search, and no question stands for
    a search the report did not measure.

## Non-goals
- No per-engine breakdown, no ChatGPT or Perplexity result on the free report
  (`BUILD.md` §6.2, "Paid only" and "Deferred to v1.1").
- No sentiment, ranking, or share-of-voice metric derived from the citations.
- No advice on how to influence what an assistant recommends (`BUILD.md` §14) —
  stated as criterion 7, not left to this list.
- No editing, reordering, or removing of individual questions on the free report
  — the only correction available is the market itself (REQ-094).
- No display of the full ~50-search market set; the report shows the 12.
- No generated commentary or interpretation attached to any question; whether
  the product's own voice may be model-written is REQ-093's rule, cited here and
  not restated.
- No phrasing of a question that changes which search it was selected from
  (`BUILD.md` §6.7) — stated as criterion 13, not left to this list.
