# REQ-023 — Checkout records the buyer's country and VAT ID

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a business buyer, I want my country and VAT number recorded against my
purchase, so that my invoices are correct for my accounts from the first
payment onward.

## Rationale
Most buyers are businesses whose bookkeeping needs a VAT identity on the
invoice, and a record that was never captured cannot be reconstructed later.
What must be true for the customer: the country of the billing address they gave
and the VAT number they entered sit on their billing record and on their
invoices, no country is refused a purchase, no registry check stands between
them and buying, and a buyer who did not have that number to hand at checkout is
never stuck with the record wrong — it stays theirs to correct at any time,
without asking anyone, on the surface REQ-076 criterion 2 owns. This requirement
owns the record and the customer's power to correct it; the surface that
correction happens on is REQ-076's and the amount payable is REQ-022's
(`BUILD.md` §13).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder at checkout, when they pay, then the country of the billing
   address they gave at checkout is recorded against their billing record, and
   no country they give refuses them the purchase.
2. Given a business buyer at checkout, when they have a VAT number, then a field
   exists to enter it, and what they enter appears on their billing record and
   their invoices.
3. Given a buyer with no VAT number, when they leave that field empty, then the
   purchase completes unimpeded; and given a buyer who enters one, when they
   submit it, then it is recorded and the purchase completes whatever any
   registry would say about that number — no check against VIES or any other
   registry stands between them and their purchase.
4. Given a buyer who entered no VAT number at checkout, or a wrong one, when
   they set out to correct it, then it is theirs to add or correct at any time
   afterwards, without contacting anyone, on the surface REQ-076 criterion 2
   sends them to; and from the moment they do it is recorded against their
   billing record and appears on every invoice issued afterwards.

## Non-goals
- Calculating, charging, displaying or remitting VAT.
- The amount payable and whether tax is inside it — REQ-022 criteria 1 and 4
  state both.
- Validating a VAT number against VIES or any registry — criterion 3 carries
  the customer's promise that no such check can block them.
- Reverse-charge or tax-status wording on invoices.
- Country-based pricing (REQ-022 criterion 4), or blocking purchases from any
  country — criterion 1 carries the second.
- Where the customer corrects the number, and every other billing control —
  plan, price, next invoice, card, invoices and cancellation — all REQ-076's.
  Criterion 4 states only that the correction is the customer's to make; it
  claims no surface of its own and adds no field to Settings.
- How what the customer gives reaches Stripe, and which of Stripe's fields
  holds it — the blueprint that owns billing decides both.
