# REQ-045 — The customer reads the whole page, its grounded fact and its claim check before it goes out

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer who is the publisher of record, I want to read the finished
page exactly as it would publish, see what it is grounded in and whether it
passed its checks, so that I can approve or stop it on evidence rather than
faith.

## Rationale
The customer's name goes on every page, so nothing may publish that they could
not have inspected in full — every word that would go out, the fact it stands on
with the page and date it was read from, and the result of the check against
what they forbid. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.6 (the draft view: full page render,
grounded-fact highlight with its source line, claim-check badge, Approve / Edit /
Veto, and the "what happens if you do nothing" box). What may be model-written
anywhere in the interface, and how it is identified, is REQ-093's.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a draft awaiting review, when the customer opens it, then every word
   that would publish is rendered for them to read, with nothing withheld or
   summarised, and what ReachKit wrote is labelled here as that page's generated
   content, in the form REQ-093 criterion 2 requires of it wherever it appears —
   "it appears as that page's content, identified as the page it belongs to".
   Where the customer has edited the draft, that label never presents their own
   words as text ReachKit generated (REQ-093's non-goal, "Text the customer writes
   themselves … which is theirs").
2. Given a draft is rendered for review, when the customer reads it, then the
   fact it is grounded in is marked within the text and shown with the URL it was
   read from and the date it was read.
3. Given a draft is rendered for review, when the customer reads it, then the
   outcome of the check against their do-not-claim list is stated — including
   where they have recorded no entries, in which case it states that there was
   nothing to check against.
4. Given a draft awaiting review, when the customer reads it, then they are told
   what will happen if they do nothing, and can approve, edit or veto without
   leaving the view.

## Non-goals
- Reproducing the appearance a page will take inside a customer's own WordPress
  theme (REQ-060) — this view promises the words, not a destination's rendering.
- Images in drafts.
- Comments, suggestions or multi-person approval.
- Publishing directly from this view to a destination the customer has not
  connected.
- Typography, highlight styling and badge tone — design system.
- How an edited draft's label distinguishes the customer's words from ReachKit's
  — the blueprint's, from criterion 1's promise that it never claims theirs.
