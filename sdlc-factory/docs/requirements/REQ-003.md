# REQ-003 — The scan reports its progress and returns a readable report within 60 seconds

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder waiting on a scan of my own domain, I want to see what is being
measured and get the answer in under a minute, so that I do not give up on a
page that looks stuck.

## Rationale
A stranger with no reason to trust us abandons an unlabelled wait, so the wait
must name what is being measured, end inside a minute, and — however slowly a
third party's server answers — end at a bounded moment in a report or a stated
failure, never in a page the visitor has to guess about. Evidence for the
sixty-second figure, the live mode it buys and the ninety-second ceiling derived
from it is in `registry/evidence/REQ-003.md`. Source: `BUILD.md` §11 (the `scan/run` job) and
§4.1 States (scanning, degraded).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a scan is running — however it started: a submission on the landing
   page, a first visit to a shared report address, or a re-measurement after a
   market correction — when the visitor watches the page, then it shows named
   stages that advance as work completes, never an unlabelled spinner or an
   indeterminate bar alone.
2. Given scans of reachable domains, when they run, then at least 95 in every
   100 of them are readable within 60 seconds of that scan starting, whichever
   of those three ways started it, and every one of them ends within the 90
   seconds of criterion 5.
3. Given a scan that completes while the visitor is watching, when it finishes,
   then the report replaces the progress view without the visitor reloading.
4. Given a scan that cannot complete at all, when it ends, then the visitor is
   told in one written line that the measurement failed and is offered a manual
   retry (the retry window is REQ-012's).
5. Given a scan still running past 60 seconds — including when the delay is the
   scanned domain's own server answering slowly — when it reaches 90 seconds,
   then measuring stops and the visitor is shown the report of everything
   measured by that point, with the rest reported as unmeasured (REQ-005). No
   visitor waits on a running scan beyond 90 seconds.

## Non-goals
- No queue position, percentage estimate, or countdown timer.
- No emailed "your report is ready" notification for the free scan.
- No background continuation for a visitor who closes the page mid-scan.
