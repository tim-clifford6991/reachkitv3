# REQ-062 — A published page is verified a day later, and the result is shown

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want the product to go and check that yesterday's page
is actually live, findable and readable by AI, so that "published" means the
page exists in the world and not just in a database.

## Rationale
A publish that reports success but produced an unreachable or unindexable page
is the failure the customer would otherwise discover weeks later, so the product
goes and fetches the page a day after it went out and shows every outcome,
failures included, rather than letting "published" mean only that a row was
written. BUILD.md §9 fixes the check — "**Verification:** publish +24h → fetch
the live URL, confirm reachable/indexable/in-sitemap/AI-readable (chips in the
day panel)" — and BUILD.md §12 fixes what the customer is sent: "`published`
(live URL + 24h checks)".

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a page published to a destination that serves it publicly, when 24
   hours have passed since it published, then its live address is fetched and
   the product records whether the page is reachable, is not marked against
   indexing, is present in the sitemap, and returns its whole page content to a
   crawler that runs no scripts and identifies as one the robots policy REQ-059
   serves permits.
2. Given verification has run, when the customer inspects that page, then each of
   those four outcomes is shown, with the date and time it was checked in the
   time zone the customer set.
3. Given any of those checks failed, when the outcome is shown, then the failure
   is stated plainly rather than omitted or shown as pending; and in the 24 hours
   before the check runs, the four outcomes state that they have not been checked
   yet and when they will be, never that they passed or failed.
4. Given a page was unpublished or taken down before its check was due, when the
   customer inspects it, then no check is run and it states that the page was
   taken down before it could be verified.
5. Given verification has completed, when the customer is told about that page,
   then one message carries its live address together with the outcome of all
   four checks, and it is sent on the same occasion whether every check passed
   or any failed — a failed check is never a reason to send nothing — naming
   which failed where any did.

## Non-goals
- Repairing a failed check automatically.
- Verifying a page delivered to a destination as a draft the customer must
  publish themselves — it has no live address (REQ-060).
- Traffic, ranking or conversion analytics for the page — the weekly verdict in
  REQ-063 is the only performance judgement.
- Re-verifying a page repeatedly after its first check.
- Whether the customer has switched the published-page mail off — REQ-075;
  criterion 5 fixes only that a failed check never changes whether it is sent.
