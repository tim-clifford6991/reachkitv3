# REQ-041 — Overview answers whether the work is working, what needs the customer today, and how far ahead each rival still is

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want the first screen I land on to tell me whether the
work is paying off, whether anything is waiting on me, and how far ahead each
competitor I confirmed still is, so that a five-minute visit is enough to stay in
control and to tell whether I am catching up rather than just moving.

## Rationale
The single question a subscriber asks every week is "is this working?", and the
default screen must answer it from measured values rather than assertion, while
surfacing anything blocked on the customer instead of letting it sit unseen.
Absolute progress is only half of that answer — closing the gap on the rivals the
customer named is the other half — and the rival module must stay truthful for a
customer who ranks for nothing, which is the state every new customer starts in
and where a ratio against zero would be an invented number. Placement: JN-003
step 1, JN-005 steps 2 and 3. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.5 (growth chart of
searches-you-appear-in by week; "max one headline number per module; every value
carries its delta or its goal, never bare"; a 7-day strip and up to two alerts;
per-rival name and falling line, "Every line pointing down is the gap
shrinking") and §6.6 (at a customer count of 0 render the rivals' absolute
numbers with `you: 0`, never a ratio; the ratio module unlocks at ranked ≥ 10).
Research on competing products' measurement practices and AI-answer stability is in
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-competitor-visibility-measurement.md` and
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-ai-answer-stability.md`.
No implementation exists to touch: this repo carries no `src/`
(`00-project.md`, "Greenfield. No source code on disk yet").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a signed-in customer, when they open the app, then Overview is the
   screen they land on.
2. Given at least one completed weekly measurement, when Overview renders, then
   it shows how many searches the customer appears in over time, with the current
   value labelled and carrying the date it was measured.
3. Given no weekly measurement has completed yet, when Overview renders, then in
   place of the chart it carries one written line saying that the first
   measurement has not run and the date it is due.
4. Given a headline number on Overview — the single number a module leads with,
   of which every module that leads with a number shows exactly one and never two
   — when it is rendered, then it carries either its change since the previous
   measurement or the goal it is moving toward, and never appears bare. Every
   headline number has a goal: a fixed value the product defines for that number,
   the same for every customer, shown with the number and what reaching it means.
   Where there is no previous measurement to compare against, the number carries
   its goal. A value a module shows for comparison or context is not itself a
   headline number and this criterion does not bind it: the rivals' absolute
   counts and the customer's own number in criterion 10, and the ratio in
   criterion 11, are such values, carry no goal, and render without one. One
   ratio is shown for each confirmed competitor rather than one for the module,
   so no ratio is the single number a module leads with: the gap module leads
   with no headline number, and this criterion binds only a module that leads
   with one.
5. Given pages awaiting the customer's review, or items that cannot proceed
   without them, when they open Overview, then at most two are named, each with a
   single control that takes them straight to it, and where more exist the screen
   states how many remain and where to see them.
6. Given the current day of the week, when the customer opens Overview, then they
   can see which days of this week are done, which is today, and which are still
   to come.
7. Given a week Overview shows whose re-measurement did not run, or ran without
   completing every measurement, when Overview renders, then that week carries the
   account REQ-065 gives it: for a week that did not run, one written line that
   this week has not been measured and the day the next one is due (REQ-065
   criterion 3); for a week measured only in
   part, the values that were measured shown with their date and the rest stated
   as not measured (REQ-065 criterion 4). In
   neither case is an earlier week's value labelled with that week's date.
8. Given confirmed competitors and a current measurement, when the customer opens
   Overview, then each competitor is shown by name with the size of the gap
   between them and the customer and how that gap has moved over time, and no
   site the customer has not confirmed as a competitor appears in the module.
9. Given the customer appears in no searches, when the gap module renders, then
   what it shows moving over time is the rival's own count, and it is not
   described as the gap shrinking.
10. Given the customer appears in fewer than 10 searches, when the gap module
    renders, then the rivals' absolute numbers are shown alongside the customer's
    own number and no ratio is displayed.
11. Given the customer appears in 10 or more searches, when the gap module
    renders, then the ratio is shown together with its previous value, except
    where there is no previous ratio it may be shown with — the customer having
    been below 10 at the previous measurement, or the previous ratio having been
    measured on the far side of a date REQ-071 criteria 12 and 13 break every
    series at — in which case it is shown with its own measurement's absolute
    counts instead.
12. Given the AI-answers headline on Overview, when it renders, then it states,
    alongside its goal, in how many of a fixed trailing window of weeks the
    customer was named in at least one tracked question's AI answer, and no
    other reading of AI-answer movement is shown anywhere on Overview: no change
    from the previous week's count, and no change reported for any single
    tracked question.

## Non-goals
- A second score of any kind, including a composite "competitiveness" score, or
  any number that answers no question the customer has.
- Model-written text anywhere on this screen — REQ-093 owns that line for the
  whole interface.
- Module order, chart geometry, tile widths and colour, sparkline geometry,
  series colours and label placement — design system.
- Drill-down analytics beyond the Calendar.
- The goal value each headline number carries — a pinned product constant
  (`00-project.md`, "`src/lib/config/constants.ts` | **Every** pinned number in
  the specification"), not a per-customer setting; criterion 4 fixes only that
  every headline number has one and never renders bare.
- Suggesting or discovering competitors the customer might add — where they
  confirm the list is REQ-070's; criterion 8 fixes only that this module shows
  nothing outside it.
- Backlink, local or per-country comparisons.
