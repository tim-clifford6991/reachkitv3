# REQ-063 — Every Monday each published page is judged against the test it was created with

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want a weekly verdict on each page I published, measured
against the promise made when it was proposed, so that I can tell what is
working without interpreting a chart and without waiting to find out that
nothing is.

## Rationale
The subscription is renewed on evidence, so once a week the customer must be
told what moved and, page by page, whether it worked, is too recent to judge, did
not work, or can no longer be judged because the test it was given has stopped
being measurable — judged against the test recorded when the page was proposed,
never against a measurement older than this week's, and with a result that went
backwards shown rather than smoothed. BUILD.md §9 fixes the judgement — "Monday
→ full re-measure; each published page gets Working / Too early / Not working
against its acceptance test. A regression is shown, never hidden" — BUILD.md
§4.5 fixes what Overview counts — "'rest under 3 weeks — too early to judge'" —
and BUILD.md §12 fixes what the mail carries: "`weekly` (score delta, AI answers
delta, pages verdicts, next 3 …)".

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a page published to a destination that serves it publicly, and a weekly
   re-measurement completed this week — the week beginning on Monday in the time
   zone the customer set — when verdicts are produced, then that page is marked
   as working, too early to judge, or not working, against the test recorded with
   its opportunity — unless criterion 6 applies, when it carries that verdict in
   place of the three; and where its 24-hour verification recorded a failure
   (REQ-062 criterion 3, "the failure is stated plainly"), that failure is shown
   beside its verdict and never in place of one, so no page goes unjudged for
   want of a check that is never run again.
2. Given a page published less than three weeks ago, when its verdict is
   produced, then it is marked as working if it already passes its recorded test,
   and otherwise as too early to judge rather than as not working.
3. Given a page whose measurement for its target search is worse this week than
   at the previous re-measurement, when its result is shown, then that decline is
   shown beside its verdict rather than hidden, rounded away or replaced by the
   better earlier figure.
4. Given the weekly re-measurement has completed, when the customer is notified,
   then they receive each page's verdict and what moved since the previous
   measurement, every figure carrying its change, the date it was measured, and —
   where the measurement it is compared against is not the previous week's — the
   interval that change spans.
5. Given a week REQ-065 criterion 3 states was not measured, when the customer
   looks at the verdicts, then no verdict is produced for any page for that week;
   and given a week REQ-065 criterion 4 states was only partly measured, then a
   verdict is produced for a page whose recorded test could be evaluated from
   what that week did measure — whichever of REQ-048 criterion 1's three forms
   that test takes, a named search, "the customer being named in the answer to a
   named question", or "a named access barrier being cleared" — and for no
   other, each remaining page stating that it was not measured this week. In
   neither case is an earlier week's verdict shown as this week's.
6. Given a published page whose recorded acceptance test (REQ-048 criterion 3)
   can no longer be evaluated — its target search is no longer measured, the
   customer unpublished the page (REQ-061), the question it named left the
   tracked set when the market changed (REQ-071 criterion 7), or the customer
   changed the domain the site is measured under, so every measurement the test
   was written against is about a different website and the page receives no
   further working, too early or not working verdict (REQ-071 criterion 14) — when
   verdicts are produced,
   then it is marked as no longer judgeable, with one written line saying which of
   those happened and the date of the last verdict it did receive — or, where it
   never received one, that it was never judged; no working, too
   early or not working verdict is produced for it that week or afterwards while
   that holds; and its recorded test is not rewritten to something that could be
   judged.

## Non-goals
- Producing a verdict from a measurement older than this week's re-measure.
- Judging a page the customer unpublished, or one delivered to a destination as a
  draft they never made live (REQ-060) — neither is live to be judged; a page
  that can no longer be judged carries criterion 6's verdict instead of one of
  the three, whether or not it ever received one.
- Re-generating or re-publishing a page automatically because of its verdict.
- No composite "content score" summarising the verdicts.
- The weekly re-measurement itself, and telling the customer when a week could
  not be measured — REQ-065's.
- Re-checking that a page is still live: the one check is REQ-062's, at 24
  hours, and its outcome never withholds a verdict.
