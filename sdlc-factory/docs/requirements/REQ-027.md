# REQ-027 — Up to five competitors, suggested or typed

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder at setup, I want to confirm who I am actually competing with —
including rivals no dataset knows about — so that the comparison I am shown
every week is the one that matters to me.

## Rationale
The weekly product is a comparison, and a comparison against the wrong rivals is
worse than none: the founder is the only authority on who they are up against,
so they must be able to type a rival no dataset knows, must not be able to
compare themselves against themselves, and must never be held at setup by the
absence of suggestions — up to the five the product tracks (`BUILD.md` §4.3,
§6.1, §6.6). What criterion 2 refuses is exactly what is refused for the same
action after setup (REQ-071 criterion 4), and resolving in DNS is a test that
the domain exists, never that the rival's site is live or substantial — a new,
thin or unreachable rival is a legitimate competitor and is accepted (REQ-071
criterion 5).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder at setup whose market is known — inferred from a report that
   measured the domain the account will use, or stated by them on the same
   screen (REQ-026) — when the card renders or the market is first stated, then
   it offers suggested rivals drawn from that market, each of which can be
   accepted or rejected individually; and where nothing has been measured for
   the domain the account will use — a purchase with no report behind it, or a
   buyer who replaced the domain the report behind their purchase measured — the
   market is the one the founder states and no rival found for the replaced
   domain is offered as theirs (REQ-090 criterion 4, "nothing measured for the
   replaced domain — its score, its questions, its market or its rivals — is
   presented as their account's").
2. Given a founder whose real rival was not suggested, when they type its
   domain, then it is added and treated identically to a suggested one; and a
   value that is not a domain name that resolves in DNS, or is their own site,
   or is already in the set however it is typed, is not added, consumes none of
   the five, and one written line says why — the same rule that governs the same
   action after setup (REQ-071 criterion 4).
3. Given a founder selecting rivals, when they have five, then no sixth can be
   added and the limit is stated on screen rather than silently enforced.
4. Given a founder who has not yet stated a market, when the card renders, then
   it says it is waiting on their market, never that no rivals were found; and
   given a market that is known, when suggestions have been sought for it and
   none came back, then one written line says none were found.
5. Given a founder with no competitors selected — none were suggested, or they
   rejected every suggestion — when they submit setup, then setup completes.

## Non-goals
- More than five competitors.
- Verifying that a typed domain is genuinely a competitor.
- Per-competitor settings, weights or notes.
- Adding a competitor to the tracked set later without the founder choosing it.
