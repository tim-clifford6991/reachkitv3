# REQ-071 — Changing the market rebuilds the question set; leaving it alone freezes it

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a customer whose category was inferred wrongly — or whose business has
moved — I want to correct the market I am measured in and be told what that
rebuilds and when, so that my weekly numbers stay comparable and a correction
never looks like a collapse.

## Rationale
The category decides the search set, the twelve questions and therefore every
number on Overview, so a customer measured in the wrong market is measured
against nothing they care about — and a customer whose questions silently
changed cannot trust a single week-on-week delta. The customer must therefore
be able to correct the category, be told before they do what that rebuilds and
when, and otherwise keep the same twelve questions week after week (BUILD.md
§6.7 step 5: the question set "**freezes after confirmation** so week-on-week
movement is comparable; it re-derives only when the category changes"; §4.7,
"changing this rebuilds the search set and the 12 questions next Monday").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when they open the market card, then the
   category currently being measured is shown, can be replaced, and is
   accompanied by one written line stating that changing it rebuilds the search
   set and the twelve questions at the rebuilding re-measurement and not before,
   and that no new page is generated in the days between.
2. Given the customer saves a different category, when the rebuilding
   re-measurement runs — the first weekly re-measurement that begins after the
   moment of the save (REQ-065 criterion 1) — then the
   search set and the twelve tracked questions are re-derived from the new
   category, the questions on screen carry the measurement date they belong
   to, and no re-measurement and no re-derivation of the question set is run
   before it.
3. Given the customer does not change the category, when any number of weekly
   re-measures run, then the twelve questions are the same twelve questions as
   the week before.
4. Given the question set was re-derived after a category change, when the
   customer views movement spanning that date, then the screen states that the
   question set changed on that date, the difference is not presented as
   movement, and each affected number carries its goal in place of a change.
5. Given any Settings change whose effect falls at a weekly re-measurement,
   when the customer looks at the product between the save and the first
   re-measurement that begins after the moment of the save, then the numbers
   shown are the ones last measured, dated, and unchanged by the edit.
6. Given a category change is saved, when the days before the rebuild pass,
   then no new page is generated from an opportunity derived under the old
   category, each of those days carries one written line saying the category
   change is why and naming the date pages resume — never the empty-pipeline
   line REQ-043 criterion 3 gives a day with nothing to publish — and a draft
   already in review keeps the window it entered with and can still be stopped.

## Non-goals
- Confirming or correcting the category at setup (REQ-026) or on the free
  report (BUILD §6.7 step 5, "Not your market? Correct it").
- Any control over how many questions there are, how they are selected, scored
  or phrased — one of the engine parameters REQ-070 criterion 3 keeps off every
  screen (BUILD §4.7).
- Editing an individual question, adding one, or removing one.
- Re-measuring immediately on save: criterion 2 carries that promise — the
  rebuild happens on the normal weekly cycle and nothing runs before it.
- Keeping history under both question sets side by side.
