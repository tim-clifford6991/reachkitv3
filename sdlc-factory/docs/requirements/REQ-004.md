# REQ-004 — The report opens with one score, its band, and the drivers behind it

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder with zero SEO knowledge, I want the top of the report to tell me in
one number and one word how findable I am, so that I know where I stand before
reading anything else.

## Rationale
A founder with zero SEO knowledge must learn where they stand from the top of
the report alone — one number, one word for what that number means, the three
things behind it, and the domain and date it belongs to — and must never be
shown a free verdict that has been reduced, blurred or held back to sell them
the paid one. The formula, its sub-measures and the band thresholds are
mechanism for the blueprint cut from this requirement. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.1
(header strip) and §5 (bands; "Identical free/paid; no tier parameter").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report whose score could be computed, when it opens, then
   the first module shows a single score on a 0–100 scale, its band as one of the
   four written words — Invisible, Hard to find, Findable, Dominant — the domain
   measured, and the date it was measured.
2. Given the score, when it renders, then the three drivers it is composed of
   are shown beside it, each named and each with its own value, or with "—" in
   place of a value the scan could not measure (REQ-005).
3. Given a report whose score could not be computed (REQ-005), when it opens,
   then the first module shows "—" in place of the score, shows no band word at
   all, still names the domain and the date, and carries one written line saying
   the verdict could not be reached and which driver is missing — no band is
   stated, implied, or guessed from the drivers that were measured.
4. Given the band, when it renders, then it is conveyed in words and not by
   colour alone.
5. Given a free report, when it renders, then no part of the score, the band or
   the three drivers is hidden, blurred, rounded down, locked, or marked as
   available on payment; the free report shows the whole verdict.

## Non-goals
- No second score, sub-score dashboard, or per-driver drill-down on the free
  report.
- No competitor score, grade letter, or ranking of the customer against a
  cohort.
- No explanation of the formula on screen; the report explains meaning, not
  arithmetic.
