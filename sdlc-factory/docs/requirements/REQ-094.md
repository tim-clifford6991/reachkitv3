# REQ-094 — The free report shows the market it measured in and lets one reader correct it

- Priority: Must

## User story
As anyone reading a free report — the founder whose own site was read as the
wrong kind of business, or the owner of a business somebody else scanned — I want
the report to show which market it judged the domain in and to let me correct it,
so that every number underneath is a measurement of the business that actually
exists.

## Rationale
The market is inferred from a homepage, so when the inference is wrong every
number under it is wrong too, and a reader who cannot see which market was used
cannot tell a measured verdict from a guess. The report must therefore show the
category on its face, offer one correction to whoever is reading it, say so
plainly when the searches themselves disagree with the category — and say
instead that coherence could not be judged where too few searches were measured
to judge it — and re-measure
against the corrected market rather than re-labelling the old numbers. Why three
measured searches is the floor below which coherence cannot be judged is derived
in `registry/evidence/REQ-094.md`. One
correction, not many: the cost bound behind that number is in
`registry/evidence/REQ-014.md`, which also carries the source clause verbatim.
That clause allows one correction per free *scan*, and criterion 6 keeps that
scope unnarrowed: a re-scan is a new free scan and carries its own correction,
the re-scan an incomplete report offers (REQ-001 criterion 14) as much as the
one age earns (REQ-001 criterion 15). What holds the bound is not a window in
which no re-scan exists, but the cost of a correction itself: because it re-runs
inside the scan it corrects, and against that scan's one spend ceiling, spending
no further scan allowance (REQ-003 criteria 6 and 11), criterion 3 states it as
the same scan continuing rather than a new one starting — so a second correction
is never cheaper than a second scan, and second scans are bounded where scanning
is bounded. Placement: JN-001 step 6 and JN-006 step 2.
Source: `BUILD.md` §6.7 step 5 ("Coherence check + correction"), whose "re-runs
steps 2–5" is why criterion 3 re-measures the market and everything resting on it
and leaves the domain's own pages alone. No implementation exists to touch: this
repo carries no `src/`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given any stored report, when it renders, then the category the report
   measured is shown, together with a control to correct it — on every report,
   whether it measured everything or only part of it (REQ-001 criterion 12), and
   not only where the market looks wrong.
2. Given a report that measured at least three searches, when it renders, then
   one written line states that the market may have been read wrongly and points
   at criterion 1's control, exactly when no single domain appears in the top ten
   of three or more of those measured searches. Given a report that measured
   fewer than three — which a scan that stopped early can produce (REQ-001
   criterion 14) — when it renders, then no line says the market may have been
   read wrongly, and one written line instead says that coherence could not be
   judged and points at that same control.
3. Given a reader who submits a corrected category, when the correction is
   accepted, then the same free scan continues against the corrected category:
   the market set, the questions, and every figure measured from them — the AI
   answers, the Google presence, and the drivers and score that rest on those —
   are measured again and the report re-renders from them, while what the scan
   read from the domain's own pages is not re-read and does not change. The
   corrected report is one scan's report throughout, carrying one measurement
   date — the moment that scan's measurement finished, correction included — and
   no figure in it comes from a different scan (REQ-004 criterion 12).
4. Given a corrected report, when any later visitor opens that domain's report
   address, then they see the corrected report — the correction replaces what was
   stored for that domain on REQ-001 criterion 17's terms — and no control or
   address returns anyone to the pre-correction report.
5. Given a report whose one correction has already been used, when any visitor
   attempts a further correction, then they are told in writing that a free scan
   allows one correction and that this one has been used, and the corrected
   report stays readable; where two corrections are submitted before either has
   been applied, the first accepted becomes the stored report and every other is
   refused the same way.
6. Given a domain re-scanned into a new stored report — the re-scan an
   incomplete report offers (REQ-001 criterion 14) or the one its age earns
   (REQ-001 criterion 15) — when that report is opened, then one correction is
   available for it: one per free scan, and a re-scan is a new free scan,
   whatever the report before it measured or failed to. Nothing but a new stored
   report renews that one: a correction never earns another correction of its
   own (criterion 5), so a second correction always costs a second scan, and the
   only scans on offer are the ones REQ-001 criteria 13 to 15 offer.
7. Given a correction whose re-measurement produced no report — it failed, or it
   was refused before it ran — when the reader returns to the report address,
   then the report stored before the correction is still there under its own
   measurement date (REQ-001 criterion 17), one written line says the correction
   did not complete, and the correction of criterion 5 has not been used: the
   category can be corrected again. A correction whose re-measurement stopped
   early does produce the stored report — incomplete, on REQ-003 criterion 11's
   terms — and that correction has been used.

## Non-goals
- No repeated or unlimited corrections on the free path: one accepted correction
  per free scan, stated as criterion 5.
- No proof that whoever corrects a report controls the domain, and no correction
  by a proven owner that outranks a stranger's or binds later free scans — both
  deferred to v1.1 with the proof of control they rest on (REQ-002).
- No editing of individual questions, searches, or rivals on the free report.
- No confirming or freezing of the market on the free path; the category is
  confirmed after payment, at setup (REQ-026), and changed afterwards in Settings
  (REQ-071 criterion 1).
- No re-reading of the domain's own pages on a correction; Foundations and
  Answerability are measured once per scan (criterion 3).

## Open questions

- [ ] REVIEW(conflict: criterion 6 vs criterion 7): criterion 6 states a second
  correction always costs a second scan, but criterion 7 leaves the allowance
  unspent whenever a correction produces no report, so a reader may attempt
  corrections again and again without a second scan. Nothing bounds the number of
  attempts, and the worst case criterion 6 rests on — two scans and two
  corrections per domain in a 7-day window — does not hold on the failure path.
  Which of the two governs is a ruling about how many times a reader may act.
- [ ] REVIEW(conflict with REQ-001 criterion 16): after a correction that produced
  no report, criterion 7 keeps the correction control available (criterion 1) and
  adds its own written line, while REQ-001 criterion 16 shows the preserved report
  with one written line and exactly one control that starts a new measurement,
  never a second control alongside it — and for a complete report under 7 days old
  REQ-001 criteria 14 and 15 leave no standing offer for that one control to be.
  Criterion 7 does not say whether a correction may be re-submitted inside that
  24-hour cooldown.
- [ ] REVIEW(conflict with REQ-003 criteria 2 and 5): criterion 3 makes the
  correction the same free scan continuing, carrying one measurement date taken
  when the correction finished, but REQ-003 criterion 2 bounds a scan's
  start-to-report time and criterion 5 stops any scan at 90 seconds — and a
  corrected scan waits on a human between its start and its report. REQ-003
  criterion 11 shares the spend ceiling explicitly and says nothing about whether
  the time ceilings are shared or start again.
- [ ] REVIEW(gap: criterion 7): criterion 7 admits a correction refused before it
  ran, but no criterion here says what refuses one. REQ-003 criterion 6 excepts a
  correction from the hourly allowance only, leaving its criterion 7 in-flight
  rule, its criterion 8 daily ceiling and switch-off, and REQ-001 criterion 16's
  cooldown unstated for corrections — so the branch has no observable trigger.
- [ ] REVIEW(conflict with REQ-006 criterion 1): criterion 2 keeps the source's
  absolute threshold of three top-ten appearances while re-basing the denominator
  from twelve to the searches the report measured. On a report that legally
  measured three to five searches (REQ-006 criterion 1), the threshold is met only
  by a domain present in all or nearly all of them, so a coherent thin market is
  told its market may have been read wrongly. Criterion 2 does not say whether the
  threshold rescales with the denominator.
- [ ] REVIEW(gap: criterion 1): criterion 1 promises the category the report
  measured together with a control to correct it on any stored report, complete or
  not, but a scan that stopped early (REQ-003 criteria 5 and 11) can store a report
  whose category was never determined (REQ-004 criteria 6 and 9). Criterion 1
  states nothing for that report — what stands where the category would be, and
  what the control corrects — while criterion 2's fewer-than-three branch still
  points its reader at that control.
- [ ] REVIEW(conflict: criterion 5 vs criterion 7): criterion 5 refuses a
  concurrently submitted second correction by telling that reader the one
  correction has been used, at a moment when the first has produced no report; if
  the first then fails, criterion 7 says the allowance was not used after all.
  Criterion 5 gives the refused reader no route back.
- [ ] REVIEW(gap: criterion 3): criterion 3 re-dates the whole corrected report to
  the moment the correction finished while forbidding the domain's own pages to be
  re-read, so the figures measured from those pages are shown under REQ-004
  criterion 1's date beside a measurement they predate. Criterion 3 does not say
  whether a figure read before the correction may carry that later date, which
  REQ-004 criterion 12 binds every number shown to.
