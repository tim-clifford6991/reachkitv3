# REQ-050 — Every draft is grounded in a verifiable fact from the customer's own pages

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer publishing under my own name, I want every page to be
anchored in something true about my own business, so that I am never the
publisher of a page that reads plausibly and says nothing I can stand behind.

## Rationale
Grounding is what separates a page worth publishing from generated filler: each
draft carries a statement that was actually read from one of the customer's own
live pages, traceable to that page and the day it was read, anything it says
about a rival is traceable to a public page too, and no figure reaches the
published page that only ReachKit holds and the reader cannot check. Source: `BUILD.md` §8 (≥1
verifiable fact from the customer's own live pages, carried with its source URL
and read date) and §8.6 (no rival metrics invented; every rival claim links its
public source).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a draft is generated, when it is queued for review, then it carries at
   least one statement of fact drawn from a page on the customer's own live site,
   stored with that page's URL, the date it was read and the passage it was drawn
   from copied word for word from the content fetched that day; the passage is
   kept unchanged from then on, so the statement can still be read against it
   after the customer's own page has moved on.
2. Given a draft for which no such statement and stored passage could be
   established, when the pipeline finishes, then the draft is not queued for review and the
   item follows the hard-rule recovery path (REQ-053 criterion 3).
3. Given a draft states any figure or claim about a competitor, when it is
   queued, then that statement links the public page it was read from, and no
   competitor figure appears that has no such page to link.
4. Given a draft is queued for review, when it is checked, then it states no
   figure that only ReachKit holds — a ranking position, a search volume, a
   visibility or answer count the product measured — so every figure a reader
   meets on the published page is one they can open a source for.

## Non-goals
- Facts about the customer sourced from anywhere other than their own live
  pages.
- Requiring a different grounded fact for every draft; repetition between pages
  is caught by REQ-054.
- Displaying the grounded fact — that promise lives in REQ-045.
- Fact-checking claims a customer writes themselves during an edit.
