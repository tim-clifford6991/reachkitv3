# REQ-060 — A customer on WordPress publishes to their existing site

- Priority: Should

## User story
As a paying customer who already runs WordPress, I want pages to land in my own
site as drafts with their metadata filled in, so that I keep the blog I already
have instead of starting a second one.

## Rationale
Most customers with a site already have WordPress, and the promise is that they
keep the blog they already have: pages land in their own site as drafts they
publish themselves, with their SEO metadata already filled in, and the product
never claims a page is live when the customer has not yet made it so. BUILD.md
§9 fixes the mechanism and its limits: "**WordPress:** REST + application
password, posts as draft + Yoast/RankMath meta when detected; credentials
encrypted at rest, never logged, revoked on disconnect."

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer connects their WordPress site with an application password,
   when a page is published to that destination, then it appears in their
   WordPress site as a draft and is never made live by us.
2. Given a page delivered to WordPress as a draft, when the delivery completes,
   then the page reaches `published` in the sense of delivered; its record names
   the WordPress site and the date of delivery and no live address, and the
   customer is told in one written line that the page is waiting in their
   WordPress for them to publish.
3. Given a WordPress site with Yoast or RankMath installed, when a page is
   published there, then the page's title and description are written into the
   fields of each of those plugins that is present.
4. Given a WordPress site with neither Yoast nor RankMath installed, when a page
   is published there, then it is still delivered, and the customer is told in
   one written line that no SEO plugin was found and the title and description
   were not written into one.
5. Given a customer's WordPress application password, when it is stored, then it
   is encrypted at rest and never written to logs or shown back to anyone.

## Non-goals
- Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, Framer or Notion.
- SEO plugins other than Yoast and RankMath.
- Installing or configuring plugins on the customer's site.
- Uploading media, or editing existing WordPress posts.
- Publishing a page live on WordPress on the customer's behalf.
- The 24-hour liveness verification, which needs a live address a WordPress
  draft does not have — REQ-062.
- Destroying credentials on disconnect — REQ-074.

## Open questions
- [ ] REVIEW(ambiguity): criteria 2 and 4 say the customer "is told in one written line" without naming a surface, and REQ-062's non-goal takes a WordPress draft out of the only occasion that sends the `published` mail (REQ-062 criterion 5). Whether a WordPress-only customer is told anywhere other than by opening the app that their page is waiting in their site for them to publish is therefore undecided, and criterion 2's line is the whole of that telling.
