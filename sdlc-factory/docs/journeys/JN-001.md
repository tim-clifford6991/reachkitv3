# JN-001 — A stranger scans their domain and leaves with a finished page

A founder who has never heard of ReachKit arrives from a shared link or a
search, gives us nothing but a domain, and wants to know one thing: when a buyer
asks AI about this market, does anyone say my name? The journey succeeds when
they have read an honest answer — including the parts we could not measure —
understood which three things are wrong and how to fix them themselves, and left
with a finished, publishable page in their inbox. Nothing on this path asks for
an account or a payment; the only trade is an email address for the page.

Where it leads: a founder convinced by this report goes on to Stripe Checkout
from the report's pricing card, and from there to a magic link and setup. That
is a different persona reaching a different outcome — a buyer provisioning an
account, not a stranger being convinced — and belongs to its own journey, not to
a branch inside this one.
