# JN-006 — The verified domain owner — correcting or removing a public report about my business

Someone the owner has never met ran a free scan on their domain and shared the
link. The report is a permanent public verdict about their business, written
without them, and it may be wrong about the market it judged them in. This
persona is not JN-001's stranger scanning their own site, nor JN-002's buyer:
they arrive with no interest in the product and one demand — that a public page
about their business is either right or gone. The journey succeeds when the owner
holds that page: it is measured against the market they actually operate in, or
it is gone from its address.

Steps 4 and 5 are not two journeys. Correction and removal are the two
dispositions of one authority, bought by the single proof of control in step 3
and open to nobody else — one persona, one outcome, decided at the last step.
Splitting them would copy steps 1 to 3 into a second journey, which is the second
copy rule 2.4 forbids. The fork JN-001 refuses is a different one: a different
persona reaching a different outcome.
