# JN-003 — The daily page — from tomorrow's plan to a page live on my domain

This is the loop the subscription is bought for and the one the customer walks
most often: a page is planned from something we measured, written the evening
before, held in review long enough for them to object, and published to a site
they own. The persona is not an SEO — they are a founder with five minutes, so
the journey succeeds when they can tell in one screen whether tomorrow's page is
right, act on it in one click, and find it live the next morning without having
checked anything else. It succeeds equally when they do nothing at all: silence
publishes on autopilot and publishes nothing in copilot, and either way they were
told first. It fails the moment a page appears they cannot explain, a retry
duplicates a post, a day is filled with a page nobody had evidence for, a day is
empty and will not say which kind of empty it is, or a page stops without saying
what stopped it.
